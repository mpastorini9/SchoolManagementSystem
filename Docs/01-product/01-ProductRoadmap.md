# Product Roadmap

## Purpose

This document defines the planned evolution of the School Management System product.

It describes the major product milestones, their strategic objectives, and the business capabilities expected to be delivered over time.

The roadmap communicates product direction rather than implementation details.

Specific features, priorities, and release dates may evolve, but every milestone should remain aligned with the project's Vision and Scope.

---

# Roadmap Principles

The product evolves incrementally through production-ready releases.

Each milestone should deliver complete and valuable capabilities before expanding into new functional areas.

The roadmap follows these principles:

- Deliver value continuously.
- Prioritize quality over feature quantity.
- Build sustainable foundations before expanding.
- Validate each milestone in real educational environments.
- Maintain consistency with the product vision.

---

# Version 0.1 — Attendance MVP

## Objective

Validate the platform through a production-ready Attendance module.

### Primary Deliverables

- Student Management
- Course Management
- Attendance Management
- Teacher Attendance Interface
- Administrative Interface
- Authentication and Authorization
- Initial Reporting
- Stable Public API

### Success Criteria

Version 0.1 is complete when:

- Attendance works end-to-end.
- Teachers can perform daily attendance efficiently.
- The API is stable and frontend-ready.
- No critical defects remain.
- The platform is validated in a real educational environment.

---

# Version 0.2 — Administrative Operations

## Objective

Simplify administrative tasks performed at the beginning of the academic year.

### Primary Deliverables

- Bulk data import.
- Student validation.
- Course assignment.
- Administrative automation.
- Data quality improvements.

---

# Version 0.3 — Student Records

## Objective

Provide a complete historical record for every student.

### Primary Deliverables

- Student Records module.
- Observations.
- Disciplinary actions.
- Parent meetings.
- Historical timeline.

---

# Version 0.4 — Teacher Management

## Objective

Support the management of teachers and their academic responsibilities.

### Primary Deliverables

- Teacher Management.
- Subject assignments.
- Course assignments.
- Teacher schedules.
- Teacher attendance.

---

# Version 0.5 — Parent Portal

## Objective

Improve communication between schools and families.

### Primary Deliverables

- Parent accounts.
- Attendance consultation.
- Student record consultation.
- Notifications.
- Password recovery.

---

# Version 1.0 — Production Platform

## Objective

Deliver the first complete production release of the platform.

Version 1.0 should provide a reliable foundation for the daily administrative operation of educational institutions.

### Primary Deliverables

- Integrated business modules.
- Stable production environment.
- Complete administrative workflows.
- Reporting capabilities.
- Multi-role user experience.
- Production deployment readiness.

---

# Beyond Version 1.0

Future evolution may include capabilities such as:

- Multi-school administration.
- Advanced analytics.
- Billing and payments.
- Document management.
- Scheduling.
- Additional communication tools.
- New business modules driven by customer needs.

These capabilities represent the long-term direction of the product rather than committed releases.

---

# Roadmap Evolution

The roadmap is intentionally iterative.

Milestones may be refined, expanded, merged, or reprioritized as the product evolves.

Roadmap changes should always remain consistent with:

- Vision
- Scope
- Product strategy
- Real customer feedback
