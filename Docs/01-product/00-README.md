# Readme

## Purpose

This section describes the product from a functional and strategic perspective.

Its purpose is to explain what the platform is expected to deliver, how it will evolve over time, and which business capabilities compose the product.

Unlike the Architecture and Backend sections, this documentation focuses on the product itself rather than its implementation.

---

# Contents

This section currently includes:

- **Product Roadmap** — Describes the planned evolution of the product through successive milestones and releases.
- **Product Modules** — Provides an overview of the functional modules that compose the platform and the business capabilities they deliver.

Additional product documentation may be incorporated in the future as the platform evolves.

---

# Reading Order

Recommended reading order:

1. Product Roadmap
2. Product Modules

The roadmap explains **when** the product is expected to evolve.

The modules document explains **what** capabilities make up the product.

Together, they provide a complete functional overview of the platform.

---

# Responsibilities

This section is responsible for:

- Defining the planned evolution of the product.
- Describing the platform's functional capabilities.
- Providing a business-oriented view of the system.
- Aligning product planning with the project's Vision and Scope.

This section intentionally avoids implementation details, architectural decisions, and technical design.
