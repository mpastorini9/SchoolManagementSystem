# DevOps Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines the overall DevOps strategy followed throughout the School Management System.

Its purpose is to establish the principles that guide how the application is built, deployed, operated, monitored, and evolved throughout its lifecycle.

DevOps is considered a continuous engineering practice that supports reliable software delivery and long-term operational excellence.

---

# DevOps Principles

The project's DevOps strategy is guided by the following principles:

- Reliability
- Automation
- Consistency
- Observability
- Scalability
- Security

Operational practices should support the engineering goals of the project while minimizing manual effort and operational risk.

---

# Automation

Whenever practical, repetitive operational tasks should be automated.

Automation improves consistency, reduces human error, and enables more reliable software delivery.

Examples include:

- Application builds.
- Deployments.
- Testing.
- Environment provisioning.
- Operational validation.

---

# Reliability

Operational processes should prioritize stability and predictability.

Deployments, configuration changes, and infrastructure modifications should minimize service disruption and reduce operational risk.

Reliability is achieved through well-defined processes rather than manual intervention.

---

# Consistency

Application environments should remain as consistent as possible throughout the software lifecycle.

Development, testing, and production environments should differ only where required by their specific responsibilities.

Consistency reduces unexpected behavior and simplifies troubleshooting.

---

# Observability

Operational visibility is essential for maintaining a reliable platform.

The system should provide sufficient information to understand:

- Application health.
- System behavior.
- Failures.
- Performance.
- Operational trends.

Observability enables informed operational decisions and faster problem resolution.

---

# Scalability

Operational practices should support the platform's long-term growth.

Infrastructure, deployment processes, and operational procedures should evolve without requiring fundamental changes to the application's architecture.

Scalability should be considered an ongoing objective rather than a future optimization.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Operational practices should evolve together with the application.

New technologies, improved automation, and lessons learned from production should be incorporated whenever they provide measurable long-term value.

Continuous improvement is an essential part of the DevOps culture.
