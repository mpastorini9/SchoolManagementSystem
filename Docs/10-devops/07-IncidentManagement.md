# Incident Management

## Purpose

This document defines the principles for identifying, managing, resolving, and learning from operational incidents within the School Management System.

Its purpose is to minimize the impact of unexpected events, restore normal service as quickly as possible, and continuously improve the platform's operational reliability.

Operational incidents should be managed through structured and repeatable processes rather than improvised responses.

---

# Incident Management Principles

Operational incident management should prioritize:

- Rapid detection.
- Effective communication.
- Timely resolution.
- Root cause analysis.
- Continuous improvement.

Every incident provides an opportunity to strengthen the platform and improve operational practices.

---

# Incident Detection

Operational issues should be identified as early as possible.

Detection may occur through:

- Monitoring systems.
- Health checks.
- Automated alerts.
- User reports.
- Operational observations.

Early detection reduces the impact of operational incidents.

---

# Incident Response

When an incident occurs, the primary objective is to restore normal service safely and efficiently.

Response activities should:

- Assess the situation.
- Reduce user impact.
- Restore service.
- Preserve operational information for later analysis.

Operational decisions should prioritize service reliability and data integrity.

---

# Communication

Operational incidents should be communicated clearly to the appropriate stakeholders.

Communication should be:

- Timely.
- Accurate.
- Transparent.
- Appropriate to the severity of the incident.

Clear communication improves coordination during incident resolution.

---

# Root Cause Analysis

After service has been restored, contributors should investigate the underlying cause of the incident.

The objective is to understand why the incident occurred and identify improvements that reduce the likelihood of recurrence.

Root cause analysis should focus on improving systems and processes rather than assigning blame.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Lessons learned from operational incidents should be incorporated into the project's engineering and operational practices.

Possible improvements may include:

- Better monitoring.
- Improved documentation.
- Stronger automation.
- Additional testing.
- Process refinement.

Operational maturity grows through continuous learning.
