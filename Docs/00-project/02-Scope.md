# Scope

## Purpose

This document defines the scope of the School Management System project.

Its purpose is to establish the boundaries of the product, ensuring that development efforts remain aligned with the project's vision, strategic objectives, and long-term direction.

The scope provides a framework for evaluating new features, architectural decisions, and future product evolution.

---

# Product Scope

School Management System is a Software as a Service (SaaS) platform designed to support the operational and administrative needs of educational institutions.

The platform provides a unified and modular solution that enables schools to manage their daily operations through a shared architecture and consistent user experience.

Each functional capability is developed as an independent module while remaining part of a cohesive and integrated platform.

---

# Initial Scope (MVP)

The first production milestone focuses on delivering a stable, reliable, and production-ready Attendance module.

The MVP includes only the functionality necessary to validate the platform in a real educational environment and establish a solid foundation for future expansion.

The initial scope includes:

- Student management.
- Course management.
- Attendance registration.
- Attendance history.
- Basic reporting.
- Authentication and authorization.
- Administrative interface.

The MVP prioritizes reliability, usability, maintainability, and product validation over feature quantity.

---

# Future Scope

After the successful validation of the MVP, the platform is expected to evolve through the addition of new functional modules.

Examples include:

- Student Records.
- Teacher Management.
- Academic Performance.
- Billing and Payments.
- Notifications.
- Reports and Analytics.
- Parent Portal.
- Document Management.
- Scheduling.
- Multi-school administration.

The order and priority of future capabilities are defined by the Product Roadmap.

---

# Out of Scope

The following capabilities are intentionally outside the current scope of the project:

- Learning Management System (LMS) functionality.
- Virtual classrooms.
- Video conferencing.
- Educational content creation.
- Student learning platforms.
- General-purpose accounting software.
- Human Resources management.
- Payroll processing.
- Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) functionality unrelated to educational institutions.

These capabilities may be reconsidered in the future but are not part of the current product strategy.

---

# Scope Management

New functionality should only be incorporated when it satisfies one or more of the following criteria:

- Supports the long-term vision of the project.
- Delivers measurable value to educational institutions.
- Fits naturally within the modular architecture.
- Preserves simplicity and maintainability.
- Aligns with the long-term product strategy.

Features that do not satisfy these principles should be carefully evaluated before becoming part of the product.

---

# Scope Evolution

The scope of the project is expected to evolve throughout its lifecycle.

Scope changes should be intentional, documented, and aligned with both the project vision and the Product Roadmap.

Significant changes should be reflected in the Engineering Handbook and, when appropriate, documented through an Architecture Decision Record (ADR).

Expanding the scope should strengthen the product rather than increase unnecessary complexity.

---

# Design Principles

Scope management follows these principles:

- Vision-driven evolution.
- Clear product boundaries.
- Incremental growth.
- Modular expansion.
- Intentional decision-making.
- Sustainable complexity.

Every addition to the product should reinforce its long-term direction.
