# Business Decisions

## Purpose

This document defines how significant business decisions are documented throughout the School Management System.

Its purpose is to preserve product and business decisions that influence the behavior, functionality, or evolution of the platform.

Business decisions provide context for why the system behaves in a particular way and help ensure that future development remains aligned with the product vision.

---

# When to Document a Business Decision

A business decision should be documented whenever it has a meaningful impact on the product's behavior or business rules.

Examples include:

- Business workflows.
- Functional requirements.
- Product policies.
- User experience decisions.
- Module priorities.
- Feature scope.

Routine implementation details should not be documented as business decisions.

---

# Decision Structure

Every business decision should include:

- Context
- Decision
- Business Rationale
- Alternatives Considered
- Expected Impact

The objective is to explain why the product behaves as it does from a business perspective.

---

# Decision Principles

Business decisions should be:

- User-centered
- Clearly justified
- Consistent with the product vision
- Aligned with the project scope
- Focused on delivering measurable value

Business decisions should prioritize solving real problems for educational institutions.

---

# Decision Evolution

Business requirements may evolve as the platform grows.

When a business decision changes, the original decision should remain documented while the new decision explains the reasons for the change.

Maintaining business history helps contributors understand the evolution of the product.
