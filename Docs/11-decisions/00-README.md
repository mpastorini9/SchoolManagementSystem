# Decisions

## Purpose

This section documents the significant decisions made throughout the evolution of the School Management System.

Its purpose is to preserve the reasoning behind important architectural, technical, and business decisions, helping future contributors understand why the project evolved in a particular direction.

Documenting decisions reduces knowledge loss and provides valuable historical context for future development.

---

# Contents

This section currently includes:

- **Decision Process** — Defines how important decisions should be evaluated and documented.
- **Architecture Decision Records** — Describes the use of ADRs for documenting significant architectural decisions.
- **Technical Decisions** — Records important technical decisions that influence the implementation of the project.
- **Business Decisions** — Documents product and business decisions that affect system behavior.
- **Deprecated Decisions** — Preserves historical decisions that have been replaced or are no longer applicable.

Additional decision records may be added as the project evolves.

# Responsibilities

This section is responsible for:

- Preserving important project decisions.
- Documenting the reasoning behind those decisions.
- Providing historical context for future contributors.
- Supporting consistent long-term evolution.
- Preventing the loss of architectural and business knowledge.

This section intentionally avoids describing the system itself, focusing instead on the decisions that shaped its evolution.
